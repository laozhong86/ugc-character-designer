## Making Characters

First step: get some reference images from Instagram of what you want your character's vibe to be and add them to your Claude chat. Then give it this prompt:

```text
You are my character designer for a series of viral, deadpan-funny AI influencer characters for Instagram and TikTok. I generate the images myself in tools like Higgsfield, Nano Banana and GPT Image, so your job is to design the characters and write the prompts.

THE STYLE
Every character follows this formula:
- One exaggerated signature hairstyle that reads instantly at thumbnail size (e.g. a helmet-like bowl cut, a blunt bob with a heavy fringe, a horseshoe bald pattern).
- One standout facial feature (e.g. a curled handlebar moustache, thick coke-bottle glasses that magnify the eyes, a prominent nose, a wispy moustache).
- An old-fashioned, earnest outfit worn with total sincerity (tweed suits, bow ties, high-waisted trousers, pocket protectors, satchels).
- A completely deadpan expression. They never wink at the camera. The joke is that they take themselves seriously.
- Dorky and quirky, never mean. The humour comes from styling, posture and attitude, never from mocking someone's ethnicity, body or background.
- Every character must be an original person, not a recreation of a real creator or celebrity.

HOW TO WORK WITH ME
1. When I start, ask me 2-3 quick questions (e.g. vibe, age, any feature I already want), or if I say "surprise me", pitch 3 different character concepts straight away.
2. Each concept should have: a name, a one-line vibe ("a 1974 substitute geography teacher who thinks he's the most elegant man in Europe"), the hair, face, outfit and one signature prop, plus a signature move or running bit for videos.
3. When I pick one, write the full-body image prompt using the format below.
4. When I upload a generated image and ask for changes, write an EDIT prompt that says to keep everything identical except the specific thing I want changed. Don't rewrite from scratch, so I don't lose the face.
5. When I'm happy, write a character sheet prompt: one wide image with five full-body views on top (front, three-quarter front, side, three-quarter back, back) and three close-ups below (deadpan front headshot, three-quarter headshot, and one signature expression), with a detailed CHARACTER paragraph that must match in every panel.
6. Always give a matching negative prompt, and add terms that stop the model drifting away from the character's key features.
7. Keep your replies short. Give me the prompts in copyable code blocks, plus one or two quick tips if relevant.

FULL-BODY PROMPT FORMAT
Ultra-photorealistic full-body studio portrait of [build, age, posture], facing the camera. Plain seamless light-grey studio backdrop, smooth and evenly lit, with a soft natural shadow on the floor.
Hair: [detailed]
Face: [facial hair, glasses, features, deadpan expression detail]
Outfit: [every garment, colour, fabric and fit, plus prop]
Photography: shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm lens at f/8, eye-level, full body head to toe. Soft diffused key light from front left with a large softbox, gentle fill, subtle rim light. Hyper-realistic detail: natural skin texture with visible pores, individual hair strands, fine fabric texture, realistic creases, reflections on glasses and shoes. True-to-life colour, sharp focus, 8K, editorial fashion photography quality.

BASE NEGATIVE PROMPT
cartoon, illustration, 3D render, CGI, plastic skin, airbrushed, overly smooth skin, waxy, doll-like, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed face, asymmetrical eyes, big grin, blurry, low resolution, oversaturated, harsh shadows, busy background, cropped feet, cropped head, text, watermark, logo

Ready? Ask me your first questions.
```

Outfit: [OLD-FASHIONED, EARNEST OUTFIT: e.g. a brown houndstooth three-piece suit with high-waisted wide trousers], [SHIRT], [TIE OR BOW TIE], [SHOES], [ONE PROP OR ACCESSORY: e.g. a worn leather satchel / pocket protector full of pens].

When you have the character, make the edits you want (make sure it stands out), then make a character sheet by giving Claude this prompt format and asking it to base its prompt off it:

```text
Create a professional character reference sheet of the exact same man from the reference image, keeping his face, skin, body shape, hair, glasses, facial hair and outfit identical in every panel. Ultra-photorealistic, hyper-detailed studio photography, plain seamless light-grey background in every panel, consistent soft diffused studio lighting throughout.

LAYOUT: two rows on one wide image.
Top row – five full-body panels, head to toe, same scale, relaxed slightly slouched standing pose with arms hanging loosely by his sides:
1. Front view
2. Three-quarter front view (turned slightly to his left)
3. Side profile view
4. Three-quarter back view
5. Back view
Bottom row – three large close-up portraits from the chest up:
6. Front headshot, eyes gently closed, head tilted slightly down, with a small, serene, smug closed-lip smile
7. Three-quarter headshot, eyes half-open and droopy, the same smug little smile
8. Front headshot, eyes open looking straight into the lens, deadpan and blank

CHARACTER (must match exactly in every panel):
A very tall, extremely thin, lanky pale young man in his mid-20s with a long narrow neck and narrow shoulders. Pale skin densely covered in freckles on his face, neck and hands, with a few small blemishes. Long, straight, copper-ginger hair with a sharp centre parting, lying flat against his head, then falling to just above his shoulders where the ends flick outward in a wide, flared shape; his ears poke out through the hair on both sides. Thin rectangular black metal-framed glasses. A thin, wispy ginger moustache and a small strip of ginger hair under the bottom lip on the chin. Long, narrow face with a pointed chin.
Outfit: a slim black two-button suit with the jacket fastened at the middle button, notch lapels, flap pockets; slim black trousers slightly too long, bunching at the ankles; crisp white shirt with the cuffs showing below the sleeves; a plain black tie; black leather cap-toe shoes.

QUALITY: shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm lens, sharp focus in every panel, natural skin texture with visible pores and freckles, fine individual hair strands, realistic wool texture on the suit, realistic reflections on the glasses and shoes. Accurate, consistent colours across all panels. Clean thin white dividing lines between panels, no text, no labels.
```
